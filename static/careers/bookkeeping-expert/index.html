Bookkeeping Expert

Hands-on Australian bookkeeping: BAS, STP payroll, and month-end in Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks. You run the books end to end and help us turn that work into a process our product team can build on. Fully remote.

Contract / hourly Fully remote Australian compliance Xero · MYOB · QuickBooks

About the role

ReqOps is building accounting software, and we need someone who actually does Australian bookkeeping and lodgement work, not someone who can only describe it. You’ll run payroll, Single Touch Payroll (STP), quarterly BAS and year-end close on real (test) books, explain every step, check and approval as you go, and document the process so our product and engineering team can automate the routine parts and keep humans on the decisions that matter.

This is practical, screen-level work. We value people who know where bookkeeping goes wrong, what to do about it, and who is allowed to sign off on what.

What you’ll do

  • Run complete Australian payroll: employee setup, rates, tax, super, leave, allowances, deductions, pay runs, journals, PAYG withholding and reconciliation.
  • Prepare, validate and submit STP reports, handle rejected submissions and corrections, and complete end-of-financial-year finalisation.
  • Close the quarter and prepare the BAS: bank and account reconciliations, GST coding, PAYG withholding and instalments, variance review, approval and lodgement.
  • Take a company from year-end close through to the tax return: reconciliations, depreciation, related-party items, tax adjustments and lodgement checks.
  • Handle exceptions: incorrect employee data, failed STP submissions, mis-coded GST, prior-period errors, amendments and late lodgements.
  • Document each process as inputs, business rules, decisions, checks and exceptions, and flag what can be automated versus what needs human review.
  • Work across Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks, and be clear about what each system automates and where a person has to step in.

What we’re looking for

  • Several years of hands-on Australian bookkeeping, including payroll, STP and BAS lodgement.
  • Working knowledge of ATO obligations: BAS, IAS, PAYG, super, TPAR, FBT and EOFY finalisation, and who is authorised to lodge what.
  • Confident in at least two of Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks Online.
  • Strong at explaining your reasoning: you can walk someone through a pay run or a BAS and say why each figure is what it is.
  • Careful with detail and with client data: you never use real credentials, TFNs or bank details in demonstrations.
  • Comfortable working remotely and communicating clearly in writing and on screen recordings.

Nice to have

  • BAS agent or tax agent registration, or experience working under one.
  • Experience writing procedures, process maps or decision trees for a team.
  • Exposure to accounting software APIs, integrations or automation tooling.

Systems

Xero MYOB QuickBooks Online ATO Online / lodgement tools STP BAS & IAS

  1. A 30-minute online screening assessment.
  2. An interview with a Senior Engineer.
  3. An interview with the Business Stakeholder / Product Owner.

If we progress after the screening, we’ll have a short online conversation before round two.
