Manual Tester

Join ReqOps as a Manual Tester and help ensure the quality and performance of our products. Three years of testing experience. Fully remote. 150 rupees per hour.

Contract / hourly Fully remote 150 INR / hour 3+ years experience

About the role

ReqOps, a forward-thinking company dedicated to delivering top-notch solutions, is seeking a skilled Manual Tester with three years of experience to join our dynamic team. As a Manual Tester at ReqOps, you will play a crucial role in ensuring the quality and performance of our innovative products. You will be responsible for designing and executing test cases, identifying and reporting bugs, and collaborating closely with our development team to guarantee seamless functionality.

We value detail-oriented professionals who are passionate about quality assurance and have a keen eye for detail. At ReqOps, we foster a collaborative work environment where your expertise will be appreciated and your contributions recognized. If you are ready to leverage your testing skills in a company that values excellence and innovation, consider joining us on our journey to deliver exceptional products and services to our clients.

What you’ll do

  • Design and execute test cases to ensure software functionality meets specified requirements.
  • Identify, document, and track software defects in a structured manner.
  • Collaborate closely with developers and product managers to resolve identified issues.
  • Perform regression testing to validate that recent code changes have not adversely affected existing features.
  • Participate in test planning and review meetings to contribute insights from a quality assurance perspective.

What we’re looking for

  • Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
  • Minimum of 3 years of experience in manual testing, with a strong understanding of software development life cycle (SDLC).
  • Proficiency in creating and executing test cases, test plans, and test scripts for web and mobile applications.
  • Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail and quality assurance.
  • Effective communication skills to collaborate with cross-functional teams and report findings clearly.
